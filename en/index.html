Features

Voice and text

Local speech recognition (Whisper) and a Ukrainian text-to-speech voice (Piper). Or just type your request into the search bar.

Echo Bar

A modular panel on a configurable grid: search, pinned apps, CPU/RAM, weather, timer, music and utilities. Rearrange everything by drag and drop.

Slash commands and workflows

Quick commands, timers, file search and launching projects in your IDE — from the bar or by voice.

Pick your brain

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, plus local Ollama and LM Studio — for when your data has to stay on your computer.

Safe commands

Actions run only from a declarative allowlist of commands with parameter interpolation.

Made in Ukraine

The interface, voice and documentation are available in Ukrainian.

How it works

  1. Summon it. Press Ctrl+Space for voice or Alt+Space for the text bar.
  2. Say what you need. “Set a timer for 10 minutes”, “find the September contract”, “open the project in my IDE”.
  3. Echo does it. The action runs in the background; the answer shows up in a widget or by voice.
